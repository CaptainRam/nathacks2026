# TalkBack Manual Acceptance Checklist

Code review and automated checks cannot replace actual TalkBack acceptance testing. TalkBack was enabled on the emulator, with actual focus and “Display speech output” recorded. Physical-device testing has not been performed, and synthesized pronunciation or audio quality has not been assessed by listening. This document preserves earlier results and distinguishes completed emulator checks from unverified boundaries. The latest focus-change verification is Round 4; preceding rounds describe their recorded versions.

## Checklist for subsequent item-by-item acceptance

Preparation: enable TalkBack and operate with left / right swipes, touch exploration, and heading navigation. Then maximize font and display sizes and repeat the key flows. Record the device, Android / TalkBack versions, pass status, and unexpected speech.

## Home and photos

- Browse from the launch page through the heading, settings, camera, import, photo guidance, allergen summary, and bottom navigation in reading order.
- Every button must have a clear name and button role. Icons, decorative dots, and duplicate badges must not receive separate focus.
- After taking a photo, importing, or cancelling selection, returning to the app must allow continued navigation. Photo loading, successful addition, and failure must have clear feedback.
- Photo preview, removal, starting analysis, and replacing a photo must all work with TalkBack. After removal, focus must not remain on a control that disappeared.
- Every photo being analyzed must have a distinct name. Double tapping must open its replacement menu and allow camera or gallery selection.

## Analysis and results

- Starting analysis must announce the current phase. Progress animation must not repeatedly interrupt speech, and cancellation must work.
- Success, no recognized text, no selected allergens, read failure, and unavailable AI must have understandable states that do not rely solely on color or icons.
- When results appear, the result heading must be locatable. The overall conclusion, each allergen, evidence, and explanations must be browsable. Expand / collapse actions and current state must be announced.
- Switching bottom tabs during analysis must not let a hidden page gain focus or interrupt speech on the current page.
- Button names for retry, rescan, replace photo, and select allergens must match their actions.

## Settings and dialogs

- The back button, settings sections, and selected section must have clear names and states. New section content must be browsable after switching.
- Allergen options must announce names and checked states. The multi-select dialog must allow selection, saving, and cancellation, returning focus to its trigger.
- Switches must announce their name, purpose, and on / off state. Activating either the entire row or the switch must change the state exactly once.
- Detection modes must announce each option's name, explanation, and radio state. Input fields must announce their labels; passwords must not be spoken as ordinary text.
- Configuration validation errors, save completion, and download start / completion / failure must have perceivable feedback. Frequent download updates must not continually interrupt speech.
- Model details must expand / collapse. Model deletion, discard changes, and similar confirmation dialogs must expose their heading, message, and every action.

## History

- Empty, loading, load-failure, and populated lists must each have a clear state.
- Records must be distinguishable, announce time and conclusion, and open details by double tap.
- Deletion must be available through the TalkBack Actions menu without relying on ordinary one-finger swipes or long presses. Confirmation, cancellation, and undo must work.
- Returning from details, deleting a record, or clearing history must leave focus in a reasonable place. Hidden content must not participate in navigation.

## OCR Test entry

- Photo selection, the enhance-image switch, model actions, result headings, and the full output must all be accessible.
- OCR / ingredient extraction must provide brief phase feedback and announce completion and errors. Long extracted text must be independently browsable.
- At maximum font size, in landscape, and on small screens, the whole page must scroll. Fixed heights must not make bottom output or top actions inaccessible.

## Global checks

- At maximum font size, text and buttons must not be clipped, and every interactive control must remain reachable by touch exploration.
- Continued navigation after system Back, app Back, dialog dismissal, rotation, and background restoration must have no focus traps.
- The system photo picker and camera are external apps. Their complete return flows with this app still require verification.
